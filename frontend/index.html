How it works

One workspace, four ways to summarize

Start with Hybrid NLP for a fast, explainable extractive summary. For a more model-driven result, switch to BERT, T5, or GPT-2 from the dashboard.

01

Tokenize & tag

Sentences and words are tokenized with NLTK and spaCy, then tagged for part of speech and named entities.

02

Score every sentence

Word frequency, POS weight, entity weight, position, and length combine into one hybrid importance score.

03

Select & reorder

The highest-scoring sentences are selected, then restored to their original order for a coherent summary.

Under the hood

Two NLP engines, one hybrid score

NLTK

Classical NLP pipeline

Sentence & word tokenization, POS tagging, and chunked named-entity recognition using NLTK's tried-and-tested toolkit.

spaCy

Statistical language model

The en_core_web_sm pipeline supplies fast sentence boundaries, POS tags, and entity spans that cross-check NLTK's output.

Extractive

Extractive summarization

Sentences are ranked with a hybrid score — frequency, POS weight, entity weight, position, and length — and the top-ranked ones form your summary.

Transformers

BERT, T5 & GPT-2

Use pretrained transformer models for embedding-based selection or an abstractive summary.
